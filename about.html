OUR HERITAGE

The Rebels Story

Founded in 1984, Fremantle Rebels Softball Club has grown into one of Western Australia’s most storied and welcoming softball communities. Based at the Shirley Strickland Reserve in Ardross, our club brings together players, families, and supporters who share a deep love for the game.

From social divisions under Friday lights to top-flight State League clashes, we take immense pride in wearing the green and gold. Above all, we value sportsmanship, respect, and making sure everyone who steps onto our diamonds feels right at home.
